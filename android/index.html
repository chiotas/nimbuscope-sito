NimbuScope for Android

The beta is ready. Two taps, in this order.

  1. 1

    Join the beta testers group

    On the page tap “Join group”. Ignore the line about permissions.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Get NimbuScope from Play

    Tap “Become a tester”, then the link to download it from Google Play.

    Get it on Play
